Unit 6: Where and when? (prepositions of place and time)

Prepositions of place
- in: inside something: The keys are in my bag. We live in Sha Tin.
- on: on a surface: The book is on the desk. There is a poster on the wall.
- under: below something: The cat is under the table.
- next to / beside: very near, at the side: The bakery is next to the bank.
- between: in the middle of two things: The library is between the park and the post office.
- opposite: across from, facing: The MTR station is opposite the shopping centre.
- in front of / behind: The bus stop is in front of our school. The garden is behind the house.
- above / below: The clock is above the door.
- near: not far from: My school is near the beach.

Prepositions of time
- at + clock times and some phrases: at 7 o'clock, at noon, at night, at the weekend
- on + days and dates: on Monday, on 1st October, on my birthday, on Christmas Day
- in + months, years, seasons, parts of the day: in July, in 2026, in summer, in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening
- before / after: Wash your hands before lunch. We play football after school.
- from … to / until: The shop is open from 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. I stayed until 8 o'clock.
- for + a length of time: I waited for twenty minutes.

Giving directions
- Go straight ahead. Turn left / right at the traffic lights. Walk along Nathan Road. Cross the road. It's on your left / right. It's at the corner of King Street and Queen Road.

Common mistakes
- "I was born on 2016." → I was born in 2016.
- "See you in Monday." → See you on Monday.
- "The party is on 7 p.m." → The party is at 7 p.m.
- "in the night" → at night (but: in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening)

Practice passage: A note from Grandma
Dear Sam,
I am going to the clinic at ten o'clock this morning, so I won't be at home when you come back from school. Your lunch is in the fridge, on the top shelf. Please heat it in the microwave for two minutes. Your swimming bag is under your bed, and your goggles are in the drawer next to the sofa. Your swimming lesson is at four o'clock. The swimming pool is opposite the library, between the bakery and the sports shop. Walk straight along Park Road and turn left at the traffic lights. It is on your right. I will be home before six o'clock in the evening.
Love,
Grandma
