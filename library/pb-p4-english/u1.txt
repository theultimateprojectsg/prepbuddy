Unit 2: Every day and right now (simple present and present continuous)

Read first: Leo's school day
Leo is ten years old and he is in Primary 4. He usually gets up at half past six. He always eats breakfast with his grandfather, and they often read the newspaper together. Leo takes the school bus at seven o'clock. He never walks to school because his school is far from his home. After school, he sometimes plays basketball with his friends. Today is different. It is Saturday, and Leo is not at school. Right now, he is sitting in the living room. He is helping his grandfather to fix an old radio. Listen! The radio is working again, and it is playing his grandfather's favourite song.
As you read, look for the simple present (gets up, takes) and the present continuous (is sitting, is helping), and how-often words like usually, always, never.

Simple present: habits, routines and facts
- I walk to school every day.
- The sun rises in the east.
- With he/she/it, add -s or -es: Mei Ling plays the piano. My dad watches the news. My brother goes swimming on Fridays. The baby cries a lot. (consonant + y → ies)
- Negatives: don't / doesn't + base verb: I don't like durian. He doesn't eat meat. (NOT "He doesn't eats")
- Questions: Do you…? / Does she…? Does your sister play badminton? Yes, she does.

Adverbs of frequency tell us how often:
always (100%) → usually → often → sometimes → seldom / rarely → never (0%)
- They go before the main verb: I always brush my teeth before bed. Tom never eats breakfast.
- They go after "am / is / are": She is always kind. We are often late on Mondays.
- Other time phrases: every morning, once a week, twice a month, on Saturdays.

Present continuous: things happening now or around now
am / is / are + verb-ing
- Look! It is raining. I am doing my homework now. The children are playing in the playground.
- Spelling: make → making, write → writing (drop the e); swim → swimming, run → running, sit → sitting (double the last letter); play → playing, read → reading (just add -ing).
- Negatives: I am not watching TV. They aren't sleeping.
- Questions: Are you listening? What is Dad cooking?
- Time words: now, right now, at the moment, today, Look!, Listen!

Which one?
- Every day / always / usually → simple present: My mum usually cooks dinner.
- Now / Look! / at the moment → present continuous: Look! Mum is cooking dinner now.

Some verbs are not usually used in the continuous form: like, love, want, know, need, believe. Say "I want a drink", not "I am wanting a drink".

Common mistakes
- "She go to school by bus." → She goes to school by bus.
- "I am play football now." → I am playing football now.
- "He always is late." → He is always late.

Key words
- usually: most of the time (通常)
- always: every time (總是)
- often: many times (經常)
- never: not at any time (從不)
- newspaper: printed pages of news (報紙)
- favourite: the one you like best (最喜愛的)
- fix: make something work again (修理)

Your task
Write about your school day (about 50 words). Use always, usually, sometimes and never. Then write two sentences about what you are doing right now.
