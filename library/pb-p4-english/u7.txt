Unit 8: Joining ideas (connectives)

Connectives join words, ideas and sentences, and make your writing smoother.

and: adds information
- I like apples and bananas. Sam washed the dishes and his sister dried them.
but: shows a difference
- The film was long, but it was exciting. I wanted to go out, but it was raining.
or: gives a choice
- Would you like tea or juice? We can walk or take the bus.
because: gives a reason (answers "Why?")
- I stayed at home because I was sick. Because it was hot, we went to the beach.
so: gives a result
- I was sick, so I stayed at home. It was hot, so we went to the beach.
(because + reason; so + result: I was hungry, so I ate a sandwich. = I ate a sandwich because I was hungry.)

Time connectives
- when: When the bell rang, the students went out.
- before / after: Brush your teeth before you go to bed. After we finished our homework, we played chess.
- while: While Mum was cooking, Dad set the table.
- then / next / after that / finally: for steps and stories.
  First, wash the rice. Next, put it in the rice cooker. After that, add water. Finally, press the button.

Other useful connectives
- also: Kitty plays the violin. She also sings in the choir.
- although: Although it was late, the children weren't tired.
- if: If it rains, we will stay indoors.
- however (start of a new sentence): The test was hard. However, I tried my best.

Punctuation tips
- Put a comma before "but" and "so" when they join two full sentences: It was cold, so I wore a jacket.
- When "because", "when", "if" or "although" starts a sentence, put a comma after the first part: When I got home, I had a shower.

Common mistakes
- "Because I was tired. I went to bed." → Because I was tired, I went to bed.
- "Although it rained, but we played outside." → Although it rained, we played outside. (don't use "although" and "but" together)
- "I was hungry so that I ate." → I was hungry, so I ate.

Practice passage: The school picnic
Last Friday was the school picnic. Everyone was excited, but the sky was grey in the morning. Mr Wong checked the weather report because he wanted to be safe. Luckily, the rain stopped before we left school, so we took the coach to the country park. First, we played games on the grass. Next, we had lunch under the trees. While we were eating, a monkey came near us, and some children screamed. Mr Wong told us to stay calm and keep our food in our bags. After that, we walked along a nature trail and looked for different leaves. Although we were tired at the end of the day, we all had a great time. Finally, we went back to school at four o'clock.
