Unit 3: Talking about the future (going to and will)

Read first: Plans for the Christmas holiday
The Christmas holiday is coming soon, and the children in 4C are talking about their plans. "My family is going to visit my grandparents in Guangzhou," says Amy. "We are going to take the high-speed train." Jason is going to stay in Hong Kong. "I am going to join a basketball camp," he says. "I think it will be very tiring, but fun!" Mrs Lee, their class teacher, smiles. "I will give you a small task for the holiday," she says. "Please write a diary about one special day." Kelly puts up her hand. "Will we need to bring photos?" "That's a good idea," says Mrs Lee. "You can stick one photo in your diary." Everyone thinks it will be a great holiday.
As you read, look for going to (is going to visit) and will (will give).

"Be going to" for plans and things we can see will happen
am / is / are + going to + base verb
- I am going to visit my cousins this weekend. (a plan)
- We are going to have a class party next Friday.
- Look at those dark clouds! It is going to rain. (we can see it coming)
- Negatives: She isn't going to join the dance club.
- Questions: Are you going to buy a new bike? What are you going to do this summer?

"Will" for decisions made now, predictions, promises and offers
will + base verb (the same for everyone: I will, she will, they will)
- The phone is ringing. I will answer it. (a decision made now)
- I think Hong Kong will be very hot tomorrow. (a prediction)
- I will help you with your project. (an offer or promise)
- Short forms: I'll, you'll, she'll, we'll, they'll. Negative: will not = won't. I won't forget!
- Questions: Will you come to my party? Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

Future time words: tomorrow, tonight, next week, next month, this weekend, in two days, soon, in the future.

Which one?
- A plan you made before → going to: We are going to fly to Taipei in July. (We bought the tickets.)
- Deciding right now → will: "It's cold." "I'll close the window."
- Guessing about the future → will (with I think, maybe, probably): Maybe robots will teach us one day.

Common mistakes
- "I will to go swimming." → I will go swimming.
- "She is going to plays." → She is going to play.
- "Tomorrow I go to the dentist." → Tomorrow I am going to go to the dentist. (or: I am going to the dentist tomorrow.)

Key words
- holiday: days with no school (假期)
- grandparents: your parents' parents (祖父母)
- high-speed train: a very fast train (高鐵)
- camp: a trip where you stay and do activities (營)
- tiring: making you tired (令人疲倦的)
- diary: a book where you write about your day (日記)
- special: different and important (特別的)

Your task
Write an email to a friend about your plans for the next holiday (about 50 words). Use going to for your plans and will for one or two guesses, e.g. I think it will be fun.
