Unit 1: Talking about the past (simple past tense)

We use the simple past tense for actions that started and finished in the past. Time words that go with the past: yesterday, last night, last week, last Sunday, two days ago, in 2024, when I was six.

Regular verbs: add -ed
- play → played, walk → walked, clean → cleaned
- Verbs ending in -e: add -d: like → liked, dance → danced
- Consonant + y: change y to i and add -ed: study → studied, carry → carried, cry → cried
- Vowel + y: just add -ed: play → played, stay → stayed
- Short verbs with one vowel + one consonant: double the last letter: stop → stopped, plan → planned, drop → dropped

Irregular verbs (learn these by heart)
go → went, eat → ate, see → saw, have → had, buy → bought, take → took, make → made, come → came, get → got, run → ran, swim → swam, write → wrote, read → read (say "red"), sing → sang, drink → drank, give → gave, find → found, feel → felt, sleep → slept, tell → told, say → said, think → thought, fly → flew, catch → caught, teach → taught, is/am → was, are → were

Negatives: did not (didn't) + the base verb
- I didn't go to school yesterday. (NOT "I didn't went")
- She didn't eat her vegetables.

Questions: Did + subject + base verb?
- Did you watch the football match last night? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
- Where did you go last Sunday? I went to Ocean Park.
- What did Tom buy? He bought a new schoolbag.

Was and were
- I/he/she/it was: I was tired after the race.
- you/we/they were: We were at the library at four o'clock.
- Questions: Were you at home yesterday? Was the film good?

Common mistakes
- "Yesterday I go to the park." → Yesterday I went to the park.
- "Did she ate lunch?" → Did she eat lunch?
- "I studyed hard." → I studied hard.

Practice passage: A day at Tai O
Last Saturday, Ka Yan and her family went to Tai O. They took the MTR to Tung Chung and then they caught a bus. The bus ride was long, but Ka Yan looked out of the window and saw green hills and the sea. In Tai O, they walked along the old streets and visited the stilt houses. Ka Yan's brother, Ka Ho, bought some dried fish for their grandmother. At lunchtime, they ate fish balls and drank lemon tea. In the afternoon, they took a small boat. "Look! A dolphin!" shouted Ka Ho, but it was only a big wave. Everyone laughed. They didn't see any dolphins, but they had a wonderful day. When they got home, Ka Yan was very tired, so she went to bed early.
