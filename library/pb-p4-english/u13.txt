Unit 14: Reading notices, posters and timetables

Notices, posters and timetables give information quickly. To answer questions, scan for the key information:
- What is it about? (the title or heading)
- When? (date, day, time)
- Where? (place)
- Who can join? (age, class, members)
- How much? (price, free)
- How to join? (sign up, call, email, reply slip)
- Rules or things to bring (must, mustn't, please bring, don't forget)

Useful words on notices
- deadline = the last day to do something; sign up / register = put your name down to join; fee = money you pay; free of charge = you don't need to pay; venue = the place; participants = people who join; reply slip = the part you fill in and return; must = you have to; mustn't = it is not allowed.

Notice 1
SUNNY HILL PRIMARY SCHOOL
Inter-class Reading Day
Date: Friday, 6th December
Time: 2:00 p.m. – 3:30 p.m.
Venue: School Library and Room 401
Who can join: All P3 – P6 students
Activities: book sharing, story-telling competition, book swap
Book swap: Bring one used storybook in good condition and take home a different one.
Prizes: The best story-teller in each level will get a $50 book voucher.
How to join: Give the reply slip to your English teacher by Monday, 2nd December.
Please note: Students must be quiet in the library. Food and drinks are not allowed.

Example questions
- Where will the Reading Day take place? (in the School Library and Room 401)
- Can a P2 student join? Why? (No, because it is only for P3–P6 students.)
- What must you bring for the book swap? (one used storybook in good condition)
- What is the deadline for the reply slip? (Monday, 2nd December)
- What can the best story-teller win? (a $50 book voucher)

Notice 2: Swimming pool timetable
HARBOUR VIEW SPORTS CENTRE – Swimming Pool
Monday to Friday: 6:30 a.m. – 12:00 noon, 1:00 p.m. – 10:00 p.m.
Saturday and Sunday: 6:30 a.m. – 10:00 p.m.
Closed for cleaning: the first Tuesday of each month (all day)
Fees: Adults $17; Children (under 14) $8; Elderly (60 or above) $8
Rules: Children under 8 must swim with an adult. You must take a shower before swimming. No running beside the pool.

Example questions
- How much does a ten-year-old pay? ($8)
- Can you swim at 12:30 p.m. on a Thursday? (No, the pool is closed from 12:00 noon to 1:00 p.m. from Monday to Friday.)
- Can you swim on the first Tuesday of the month? (No, the pool is closed all day for cleaning.)
- What must a six-year-old do? (swim with an adult)
- Write one rule about safety. (No running beside the pool.)

Key words
- deadline: the last day to do something (截止日期)
- register: put your name down to join (報名)
- fee: money you pay (費用)
- venue: the place where something happens (場地)
- free of charge: you don't need to pay (免費)
- participant: a person who joins (參加者)
- timetable: a list of times (時間表)

Your task
Write a notice for a class event, like a toy swap or a sports day (about 50 words). Include the name of the event, date, time, venue, what to bring, one rule with must, and how to register.
