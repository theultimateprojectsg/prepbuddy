Unit 11: Writing (diary, email and story)

In this unit you will learn to:
- write a diary, an email, a short story and a notice
- check your writing before you finish

1. Diary entry
A diary is personal. It tells what happened and how you felt.
- Start with the date: Saturday, 9th November
- Begin with "Dear Diary," (optional)
- Use the past tense for what happened, and give feelings: I felt nervous / excited / proud.
- Answer: What happened? Where? Who with? How did you feel? What did you learn?
Model:
Saturday, 9th November
Dear Diary,
Today I took part in my first swimming competition. Before the race, my legs were shaking because I was so nervous. When the whistle blew, I jumped into the water and swam as fast as I could. I finished third! My coach said I did very well. I felt proud of myself. Next time, I will practise harder and try to win a medal.

2. Email to a friend
- To: (email address)  Subject: (a short title, e.g. "My birthday party")
- Greeting: Hi Ben, / Dear Ben,
- Opening: How are you? Thanks for your last email.
- Body: give the information or news in short paragraphs.
- Closing: Please write back soon. / See you on Saturday.
- Sign-off: Best wishes, / Love, / Your friend, + your name
Model:
Subject: Come to my birthday party!
Hi Ben,
How are you? I'm having a birthday party on Sunday, 24th November, from 2 p.m. to 5 p.m. It will be at my flat in Kowloon Bay. We are going to play board games and eat pizza. My mum is making a chocolate cake too! Please tell me if you can come.
See you soon,
Max

3. A short story
- Beginning: Who? Where? When? (One Sunday morning, Lily and her dog Coco went to the beach.)
- Problem: Something goes wrong (Suddenly, Coco ran away after a ball.)
- Events: What did the characters do? (Lily looked everywhere and asked a lifeguard for help.)
- Ending: How was the problem solved, and how did they feel? (They found Coco playing with a little boy. Lily was so relieved.)
- Use time connectives (first, then, suddenly, after that, finally), interesting adjectives (huge, tiny, sparkling), and show feelings.

4. Notice / poster
- Title in big letters, what, when, where, who can join, how to join, contact.
Model:
SCHOOL BOOK FAIR
Come and buy new storybooks at great prices!
Date: 2nd–4th December  Time: 12:30–1:30 p.m.  Place: School Hall
All students and parents are welcome.

Checklist before you finish
- Did I use the correct tense? Did I use capital letters and full stops? Did I check my spelling? Did I use connectives and describe feelings?

Key words
- diary: a book where you write about your day (日記)
- email: a message you send on a computer or phone (電郵)
- subject: the title of an email (主旨)
- invite: ask someone to come (邀請)
- notice: a short text that gives information (通告)
- story: a text about what happened to characters (故事)
- ending: the last part (結局)

Your task
Choose ONE: (a) a diary about a rainy Sunday, (b) an email inviting a friend to your birthday party, or (c) a short story that starts "One morning, I found a small box outside my door." Write about 60–80 words.
