KEY TERMS
The line with equation Ax + By = C that separates the region where Ax + By > C from the region
where Ax + By < C .
A geoboard is a board with a grid of pegs on it.
The graph of a linear equation Ax + By = C is a straight line. Every point on the line is a
solution of the equation. Every solution of this equation is a point on this line.
A horizontal line is the graph of an equation of the form y = b . The line passes through the y-axis at
(0, b) .
The points where a line crosses the x- axis and the y- axis are called the intercepts of the line.
A linear equation is of the form Ax + By = C , where A and B are not both zero, is called a linear
equation in two variables.
An inequality that can be written in one of the following forms:
Ax + By > C Ax + By ≥ C Ax + By < C Ax + By ≤ C
where A and B are not both zero.
A negative slope of a line goes down as you read from left to right.
An ordered pair (x, y) gives the coordinates of a point in a rectangular coordinate system.
The point (0, 0) is called the origin. It is the point where the x-axis and y-axis intersect.
Lines in the same plane that do not intersect.
Lines in the same plane that form a right angle.
The point–slope form of an equation of a line with slope m and containing the point (x1, y1) is
y − y1 = m(x − x1) .
A positive slope of a line goes up as you read from left to right.
The x-axis and the y-axis divide a plane into four regions, called quadrants.
A grid system is used in algebra to show a relationship between two variables; also
called the xy-plane or the ‘coordinate plane’.
The rise of a line is its vertical change.
The run of a line is its horizontal change.
The slope of the line between two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) is m = y2 − y1
x2 − x1 .
The slope of a line is m = rise
run . The rise measures the vertical change and the run measures the
horizontal change.
The slope–intercept form of an equation of a line with slope m and
y-intercept, (0, b) is, y = mx + b .
An ordered pair (x, y) is a solution to a linear inequality the inequality is true when we
substitute the values of x and y.
A vertical line is the graph of an equation of the form x = a . The line passes through the x-axis at (a, 0) .

◦ On the x-axis, y = 0 . Points with a y-coordinate equal to 0 are on the x-axis, and have coordinates (a, 0) .

• Solution of a Linear Equation
◦ An ordered pair (x, y) is a solution of the linear equation Ax + By = C , if the equation is a true
statement when the x- and y- values of the ordered pair are substituted into the equation.

4.4 Understand Slope of a Line
• Find the Slope of a Line from its Graph using m = rise
run
Locate two points on the line whose coordinates are integers.
Starting with the point on the left, sketch a right triangle, going from the first point to the second point.
Count the rise and the run on the legs of the triangle.
Take the ratio of rise to run to find the slope.
• Graph a Line Given a Point and the Slope
Plot the given point.
Use the slope formula m = rise
run to identify the rise and the run.
Starting at the given point, count out the rise and run to mark the second point.
Connect the points with a line.
• Slope of a Horizontal Line
◦ The slope of a horizontal line, y = b , is 0.
• Slope of a vertical line
◦ The slope of a vertical line, x = a , is undefined
