Unit 10: Adding and subtracting fractions (same denominator)

In this unit you will learn to:
- add and subtract fractions with the same denominator
- add and subtract mixed numbers
- do mixed addition and subtraction of three fractions
- give answers in their lowest terms

Let's start
Amy drinks 3/8 of a bottle of juice in the morning and 2/8 in the afternoon. How much does she drink in total? 3/8 + 2/8.

Adding fractions with the same denominator
Add the numerators. Keep the denominator the same.
- 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8
- 2/9 + 4/9 = 6/9 = 2/3 (reduce to lowest terms)
- 5/6 + 4/6 = 9/6 = 3/2 = 1 1/2 (change to a mixed number)
Do NOT add the denominators: 3/8 + 2/8 is NOT 5/16.

Subtracting fractions with the same denominator
Subtract the numerators. Keep the denominator.
- 7/10 − 3/10 = 4/10 = 2/5
- 1 − 3/7 = 7/7 − 3/7 = 4/7

Adding mixed numbers
Add the whole numbers, then add the fractions.
Example 1: 2 3/5 + 1 4/5
= 3 7/5 (whole numbers 2 + 1, fractions 3/5 + 4/5)
= 3 + 1 2/5
= 4 2/5

Subtracting mixed numbers
Example 2: 5 7/9 − 2 4/9
= 3 3/9
= 3 1/3

Example 3: 4 1/6 − 1 5/6 (1/6 is smaller than 5/6, so borrow 1)
= 3 7/6 − 1 5/6
= 2 2/6
= 2 1/3
Or change both to improper fractions: 25/6 − 11/6 = 14/6 = 7/3 = 2 1/3.

Three fractions at once
Work from left to right.
Example 4: 7/12 + 11/12 − 5/12
= 18/12 − 5/12
= 13/12
= 1 1/12

Example 5: 3 − 1 2/7 − 4/7
= 2 7/7 − 1 2/7 − 4/7
= 1 5/7 − 4/7
= 1 1/7

Word problems
Example 6: A rope is 4 3/8 m long. Tom cuts off 1 5/8 m. How long is the rope left?
4 3/8 − 1 5/8
= 3 11/8 − 1 5/8
= 2 6/8
= 2 3/4
Answer: The rope left is 2 3/4 m long.

Estimate to check: 4 3/8 is about 4 and 1 5/8 is about 2, so the answer should be about 2.

Common mistakes
- Adding or subtracting the denominators.
- Forgetting to reduce the answer to its lowest terms.
- Leaving an improper fraction inside a mixed number, like 3 7/5. Change it to 4 2/5.

Challenge (for fast learners)
- Fill in the box: 2 □/9 + 1 5/9 = 4 1/9. (The fractions must make 1 1/9, so □ = 5.)
- A tank is full. 3/10 of the water is used on Monday and 4/10 on Tuesday. What fraction is left? (3/10)

Key words
- add / sum: put together (加／和)
- subtract / difference: take away (減／差)
- same denominator: the bottom numbers are the same (同分母)
- borrow: take 1 whole from the whole number part (借1)
- lowest terms: the simplest form (最簡分數)
- in total: altogether (總共)
- left: remaining (剩下)
