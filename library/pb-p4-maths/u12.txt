Unit 13: Quadrilaterals (rhombuses and parallelograms)

In this unit you will learn to:
- know the properties of a rhombus
- draw and make rhombuses
- see how squares, rectangles, rhombuses and parallelograms are related

Let's start
Look at the diamond shapes on a playing card or on some road signs. Many of them are rhombuses: four equal sides, but the corners are not always right angles.

Quadrilaterals you already know
A quadrilateral is a shape with 4 straight sides.
- Square: 4 equal sides, 4 right angles.
- Rectangle: opposite sides equal, 4 right angles.
- Parallelogram: 2 pairs of parallel sides; opposite sides are equal.
- Trapezium: only 1 pair of parallel sides.
Parallel lines are always the same distance apart. They never meet, like railway tracks.

The rhombus
A rhombus has:
- 4 equal sides
- 2 pairs of parallel sides (opposite sides are parallel)
- opposite angles that are equal
Check for a rhombus: measure all 4 sides with a ruler. If they are all equal, it is a rhombus.

Drawing and making rhombuses
- On dot paper or grid paper: from a starting dot, move the same "step" (for example 2 right and 1 up) for each side, so all 4 sides are equal.
- With 4 straws of the same length joined at the ends, you can push the shape into many different rhombuses.
- With paper: fold a square of paper in half and in half again, cut off a corner at a slant, and open it out.

How the shapes are related
- Every square is a rectangle (it has 4 right angles).
- Every square is a rhombus (it has 4 equal sides).
- Squares, rectangles and rhombuses are all parallelograms (each has 2 pairs of parallel sides).
- A rectangle is not always a square. A rhombus is not always a square. A parallelogram is not always a rhombus.
A square is a special rhombus with right angles. A square is also a special rectangle with 4 equal sides.

Telling them apart
Ask these questions:
1. Are there 2 pairs of parallel sides? If not, it is not a parallelogram.
2. Are all 4 sides equal? If yes, it is a rhombus (or a square).
3. Are all 4 angles right angles? If yes, it is a rectangle (or a square).
4. Both 2 and 3? It is a square.

Common mistakes
- Thinking a rhombus must be "tilted". A square turned on its corner is still a square (and still a rhombus).
- Saying a square is not a rectangle. It is!
- Mixing up a rhombus and a parallelogram: a parallelogram only needs opposite sides equal; a rhombus needs all 4 sides equal.

Challenge (for fast learners)
- A rhombus has a perimeter of 36 cm. How long is each side? (36 ÷ 4 = 9 cm)
- True or false: "Some rhombuses are rectangles." (True: squares.)

Key words
- quadrilateral: a shape with 4 straight sides (四邊形)
- rhombus: a quadrilateral with 4 equal sides (菱形)
- parallelogram: a quadrilateral with 2 pairs of parallel sides (平行四邊形)
- trapezium: a quadrilateral with only 1 pair of parallel sides (梯形)
- parallel: always the same distance apart, never meeting (平行)
- right angle: a square corner, like the corner of a book (直角)
- opposite sides: sides facing each other (對邊)
