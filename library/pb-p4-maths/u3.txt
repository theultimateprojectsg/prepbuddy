Unit 4: Multiples and factors

In this unit you will learn to:
- find the multiples of a number
- find all the factors of a number
- understand how factors and multiples are linked

Let's start
Cupcakes come in boxes of 6. You can buy 6, 12, 18, 24, … cupcakes. These are the multiples of 6.

Multiples
Multiples of a number are what you get when you multiply it by 1, 2, 3, 4, …
- Multiples of 5: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, …
- The first multiple of 7 is 7, the second is 14, the third is 21, the fourth is 28.
- The list of multiples never ends.
- Every number is a multiple of itself (8 = 8 × 1).

Factors
A factor of a number divides it with no remainder.
- 12 ÷ 3 = 4, so 3 is a factor of 12.
- 12 ÷ 5 = 2 R 2, so 5 is NOT a factor of 12.
- 1 is a factor of every number. Every number is a factor of itself.
- A number has only a few factors. You can list them all.

Finding all the factors: work in pairs
Find all the factors of 36:
- 1 × 36
- 2 × 18
- 3 × 12
- 4 × 9
- (5 does not divide 36)
- 6 × 6 (stop when the pairs meet)
Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36.
Write them in order from smallest to largest.

Example: factors of 40
1 × 40, 2 × 20, 4 × 10, 5 × 8 → 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 10, 20, 40.

Factors and multiples go together
Because 51 ÷ 3 = 17 with no remainder:
- 3 is a factor of 51, and
- 51 is a multiple of 3.
In the same way, 4 × 9 = 36 tells us that 4 and 9 are factors of 36, and 36 is a multiple of 4 and of 9.

Is it a factor or a multiple?
- "Is 8 a factor of 56?" 56 ÷ 8 = 7. Yes.
- "Is 45 a multiple of 6?" 45 ÷ 6 = 7 R 3. No.
Use the divisibility tests from Unit 3 to help: 135 ends in 5, so 5 is a factor of 135.

Common mistakes
- Mixing up the words: factors are small (they go INTO the number); multiples are big (they come FROM the number).
- Forgetting 1 or the number itself in a list of factors.
- Missing a pair: check every number from 1 upwards until the pairs meet.

Challenge (for fast learners)
- Which numbers below 50 have exactly 3 factors? (4, 9, 25, 49: they are square numbers of prime numbers)
- I am a multiple of 7. I am between 50 and 60. What am I? (56)

Key words
- multiple: the result of multiplying a number by 1, 2, 3, … (倍數)
- factor: a number that divides another with no remainder (因數)
- first multiple: the number itself (第一個倍數)
- pair of factors: two factors that multiply to make the number (一對因數)
- list: write in order (列出)
- divides: goes into exactly (整除)
- smallest / largest: least / greatest (最小／最大)
