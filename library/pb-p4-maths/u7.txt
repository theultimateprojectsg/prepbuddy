Unit 8: Mixed operations and the unitary method

In this unit you will learn to:
- do the four operations in the right order, with and without brackets
- use the distributive property to make calculations quicker
- solve problems by first finding the value of one (the unitary method)

Let's start
Ken buys 3 notebooks at 12 dollars each and a pen for 8 dollars. He pays 3 × 12 + 8. Do we add first or multiply first?

The order of operations
1. Brackets ( ) first.
2. Then × and ÷, working from left to right.
3. Then + and −, working from left to right.
- 3 × 12 + 8 = 36 + 8 = 44
- 3 × (12 + 8) = 3 × 20 = 60
- 48 ÷ 6 × 2 = 8 × 2 = 16 (left to right, NOT 48 ÷ 12)
- 50 − 20 + 5 = 30 + 5 = 35 (left to right, NOT 50 − 25)

Step-by-step working
Write one step on each line, with "=" at the start of each line.
Example 1: 72 + 36 ÷ 4 × 3
= 72 + 9 × 3
= 72 + 27
= 99

Example 2: (25 + 15) × 6 − 84 ÷ 7
= 40 × 6 − 84 ÷ 7
= 240 − 12
= 228

Example 3: two pairs of brackets
(2 + 3) × (5 + 3)
= 5 × 8
= 40

The distributive property: a quick way
23 × 17 + 23 × 13: both parts have "23 ×", so
= 23 × (17 + 13)
= 23 × 30
= 690
It also works the other way: 15 × 12 = 15 × (10 + 2) = 150 + 30 = 180.
And with subtraction: 46 × 99 = 46 × (100 − 1) = 4600 − 46 = 4554.

The unitary method: find the value of ONE first
Example 4: 5 pencils cost 30 dollars. How much do 8 pencils cost?
- 1 pencil: 30 ÷ 5 = 6 dollars
- 8 pencils: 6 × 8 = 48 dollars
In one number sentence: 30 ÷ 5 × 8 = 48.
Answer: 8 pencils cost 48 dollars.

Example 5: A machine makes 360 masks in 4 minutes. How many masks does it make in 7 minutes?
360 ÷ 4 × 7
= 90 × 7
= 630
Answer: It makes 630 masks.

Word problems with more than one step
Example 6: Mum has 200 dollars. She buys 4 kg of apples at 18 dollars per kg and a fish for 65 dollars. How much money is left?
200 − 18 × 4 − 65
= 200 − 72 − 65
= 128 − 65
= 63
Answer: 63 dollars are left.

Common mistakes
- Working from left to right when there is × or ÷ later: 10 + 5 × 2 = 20, NOT 30.
- Doing ÷ before × when × comes first: 48 ÷ 6 × 2 = 16, NOT 4.
- Forgetting to carry the rest of the line down to the next step.

Challenge (for fast learners)
- Put in one pair of brackets to make it true: 6 + 4 × 5 − 2 = 48. ((6 + 4) × 5 − 2 = 48)
- Does division have a distributive property? (48 + 24) ÷ 6 = 8 + 4 = 12. ✓ But 48 ÷ (6 + 2) is NOT 48 ÷ 6 + 48 ÷ 2.

Key words
- brackets: the signs ( ) that show what to do first (括號)
- operation: +, −, × or ÷ (運算)
- mixed operations: more than one kind of operation in a calculation (四則混合計算)
- from left to right: in reading order (由左至右)
- distributive property: a × b + a × c = a × (b + c) (分配性質)
- unitary method: find the value of one, then of many (先求一個單位的值)
- each: for every one (每)
