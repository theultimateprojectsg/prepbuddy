Unit 12: Adding and subtracting decimals

In this unit you will learn to:
- add and subtract decimals with up to 2 decimal places
- add and subtract three numbers
- solve money and measurement problems with decimals

Let's start
A sandwich costs 18.5 dollars and a drink costs 7.8 dollars. How much do they cost together? 18.5 + 7.8.

Line up the decimal points
Write the numbers in column form with the decimal points in a straight line. Then add or subtract as with whole numbers, and put the decimal point in the answer straight below.
Example 1: 18.5 + 7.8
  18.5
+  7.8
= 26.3
(5 + 8 = 13 tenths: write 3, carry 1 to the ones.)

Fill empty places with zeros
Example 2: 6.4 + 2.75
Write 6.4 as 6.40.
  6.40
+ 2.75
= 9.15

Example 3: 9 − 3.65
Write 9 as 9.00.
  9.00
− 3.65
= 5.35

Subtracting with borrowing
Example 4: 12.3 − 4.86
  12.30
−  4.86
=  7.44

Three numbers
Example 5: 15.6 + 4.25 − 7.8
= 19.85 − 7.8
= 12.05

Example 6: 20 − 3.5 − 6.75
= 16.5 − 6.75
= 9.75

Quick ways: change the order
Addition can be done in any order. Look for numbers that make whole numbers.
- 3.75 + 4.6 + 1.25 = (3.75 + 1.25) + 4.6 = 5 + 4.6 = 9.6
- 8.4 + 2.9 + 1.6 = (8.4 + 1.6) + 2.9 = 10 + 2.9 = 12.9

Word problems
Example 7: Mum has 100 dollars. She spends 32.5 dollars on vegetables and 45.8 dollars on meat. How much money is left?
100 − 32.5 − 45.8
= 67.5 − 45.8
= 21.7
Answer: 21.7 dollars are left (21 dollars 70 cents).

Example 8: Ming jumps 2.35 m. Kit jumps 0.4 m further. How far does Kit jump?
2.35 + 0.4 = 2.75
Answer: Kit jumps 2.75 m.

Estimate to check
18.5 + 7.8 is about 19 + 8 = 27. Our answer 26.3 is close.

Common mistakes
- Lining up the last digits instead of the decimal points: 6.4 + 2.75 is NOT 2.75 + 0.64.
- Forgetting the decimal point in the answer.
- In 9 − 3.65, writing 3.65 − 9 or forgetting that 9 = 9.00.

Challenge (for fast learners)
- Two numbers add up to 10. One of them is 3.48. What is the other? (6.52)
- Fill in the box: □ − 2.75 = 4.5. (7.25)

Key words
- decimal point: the dot in a decimal (小數點)
- line up: put in a straight line (對齊)
- column form: working in columns (直式)
- carry: move to the next place (進位)
- borrow: take 1 from the next place (借位)
- further: a longer distance (更遠)
- altogether: in total (一共)
