Unit 3: Divisibility (tests for 2, 3, 5 and 10)

In this unit you will learn to:
- understand what "divisible by" means
- use quick tests to see if a number is divisible by 2, 3, 5 or 10
- tell odd numbers from even numbers

Let's start
Mrs Wong has 72 stickers. Can she share them equally among 3 children with none left over? We can check quickly without dividing.

What does "divisible" mean?
A number is divisible by another number if there is no remainder.
- 72 ÷ 3 = 24 with no remainder, so 72 is divisible by 3.
- 50 ÷ 3 = 16 R 2, so 50 is NOT divisible by 3.
Whole numbers are 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, … (we do not use negative numbers in primary school).

Test for 2: look at the ones digit
A number is divisible by 2 if its ones digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8.
- 136 ends in 6, so it is divisible by 2.
- 245 ends in 5, so it is not.

Odd and even numbers
- Even numbers are divisible by 2: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, …
- Odd numbers are not divisible by 2: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, …
- 3584 is even. 1027 is odd.

Test for 5: look at the ones digit
A number is divisible by 5 if its ones digit is 0 or 5.
- 345 and 780 are divisible by 5. 452 is not.

Test for 10: look at the ones digit
A number is divisible by 10 if its ones digit is 0.
- 780 is divisible by 10. 785 is not.
- Every number divisible by 10 is also divisible by 2 and by 5.

Test for 3: add up the digits
A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3.
- 72: 7 + 2 = 9. 9 is divisible by 3, so 72 is divisible by 3. (72 ÷ 3 = 24)
- 418: 4 + 1 + 8 = 13. 13 is not divisible by 3, so 418 is not.
- 5121: 5 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 9, so 5121 is divisible by 3.
Note: for 3, the ones digit alone does NOT tell you. 13 ends in 3 but is not divisible by 3.

Using more than one test
Is 690 divisible by 2, 3, 5 and 10?
- Ends in 0: divisible by 2, 5 and 10.
- 6 + 9 + 0 = 15, and 15 is divisible by 3: divisible by 3.
So 690 is divisible by all four.

Common mistakes
- Using the ones digit for the test for 3. You must add all the digits.
- Thinking 0 is odd. 0 is an even number.
- Saying a number ending in 5 is divisible by 10. It must end in 0.

Challenge (for fast learners)
- Fill in the box so that 4□7 is divisible by 3. (4 + □ + 7 must be 12, 15 or 18, so □ can be 1, 4 or 7.)
- What is the smallest 3-digit number divisible by both 3 and 5? (105)

Key words
- divisible: can be divided with no remainder (整除)
- test: a quick way to check (方法 / 測試)
- ones digit: the last digit of a whole number (個位數字)
- sum of the digits: add all the digits together (各數位數字之和)
- odd number: a number not divisible by 2 (單數)
- even number: a number divisible by 2 (雙數)
- whole number: 0, 1, 2, 3, … (整數)
