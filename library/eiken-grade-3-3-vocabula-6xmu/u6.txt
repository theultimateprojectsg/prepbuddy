Writing — How to write かきかた
There are two types. 2 つのタイプがあります。
1) Opinion 意見 — your opinion + two reasons (25–35 words). 意見と理由 2 つ。
Q. What is your favorite country?
• My favorite country is Japan. 意見
• First, Japan has four seasons. It's beautiful. 理由 1
• Second, I like Japanese food such as sushi. 理由 2
• So, my favorite country is Japan. まとめ
2) Email メール — answer the two underlined questions (15–25 words). 下線の質問 2 つに答える。
Friend: ... What book did you read? and How many hours did you stay?
You: I read Harry Potter for three hours. I can't wait to share more!
→ answers Q1 (what book) and Q2 (how many hours).
Write first by yourself, then the teacher gives a comment. まず自分で書いて、先生にコメントをもらおう。
