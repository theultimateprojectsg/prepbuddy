Immunodeficiency increases an individual's
susceptibility to infections and cancers.
Hypersensitivities are misdirected responses either to
harmless foreign particles, as in the case of allergies, or
to host factors, as in the case of autoimmunity.
Reactions to self components may be the result of
molecular mimicry.
