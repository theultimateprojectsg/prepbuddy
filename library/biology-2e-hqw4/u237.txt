Chapter Summary
27.1 Features of the Animal Kingdom
Animals constitute an incredibly diverse kingdom of
organisms. Although animals range in complexity from
simple sea sponges to human beings, most members
of the animal kingdom share certain features. Animals
are eukaryotic, multicellular, heterotrophic organisms
that ingest their food and usually develop into motile
creatures with a fixed body plan. A major characteristic
unique to the animal kingdom is the presence of
differentiated tissues, such as nerve, muscle, and
connective tissues, which are specialized to perform
specific functions. Most animals undergo sexual
reproduction, leading to a series of developmental
embryonic stages that are relatively similar across the
animal kingdom. A class of transcriptional control
genes called
